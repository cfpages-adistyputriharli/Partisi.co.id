## Scope

This catalog expands every parent topic in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md` into eight mutually distinct briefs. It plans editorial assets; it does not authorize automatic publication, fabricate evidence, or replace the required audit of existing routes.

## Domain boundaries

- Same-domain anti-cannibalization applies only among pages on **partisi.co.id**, including proposed articles and existing routes.
- Other owned domains may independently cover the same entity, query, or point of view. Such overlap is allowed and is not entered as cannibalization.
- Existing commercial routes retain transactional intent. Educational pages explain a reader job and link to /kontak/ only when contextually useful.
- Indonesia is the default context. No location page is created by swapping place names; local substance requires verified local evidence.
- Safety, standards, price, licensing, service coverage, and case claims remain unverified until supported by current primary evidence.

Global repeated-stem rules:

- `panduan dasar` owns definition/anatomy only; it must not rank-target comparison, cost, installation, diagnosis, or replacement.
- `cara menentukan kebutuhan` owns requirement-setting before a shortlist; `perbandingan opsi` owns side-by-side choice among a defined shortlist.
- `cara mengukur dan menyusun anggaran` owns inputs, quantities, assumptions, and cost structure; the commercial route owns a project-specific quote.
- `tahapan pekerjaan` owns sequence and hold points; `checklist inspeksi` owns recurring post-handover care.
- `diagnosis masalah` owns symptom-to-cause isolation; `kapan diperbaiki...` owns the repair/retrofit/replace decision after condition is understood.
- Existing commercial landing pages remain transaction owners unless a live evidence review explicitly changes that decision.
